## Absolute maximum vs our conditions

The SSD1680 gate rails are ≈ +20 V / −20 V (PREVGH/PREVGL, UNVERIFIED exact values — the GDEY029T94
spec does not list them in text): D23/D24 see up to ≈ 20–25 V reverse, within 30 V ✓ (Good
Display uses the same part [GDEY029T94 p29]).

## Pinout vs footprint

`D_SOD-123`: pad 1 = K, pad 2 = A. D22: 1 EPD_PREVGH, 2 EPD_SW ✓ (A = SW); D23: 1 GND, 2 EPD_CP
✓ (A = CP); D24: 1 EPD_CP, 2 EPD_PREVGL ✓ (A = PREVGL). Matches the reference circuit D3/D2/D1
[GDEY029T94 p29].
