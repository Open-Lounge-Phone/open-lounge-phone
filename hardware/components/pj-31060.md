## Pinout vs footprint

The HOOYA drawing numbers the terminals 1–6 (1 sleeve, 2 tip, 3/5 and 4/6 contact/switch pairs
per its schematic); the KiCad footprint names the same six pads T, TN, R1, R1N, R2, S. The pad
geometry matches on the drawing, but the **mapping of HOOYA 1–6 to the KiCad names and the height
of the plug axis above the mounting face are UNVERIFIED**: measure a sample (continuity with a
TRRS plug inserted and removed) before the H6 layout freezes (open item in SCHEMATIC_REVIEW.md).

## Circuit (H5)

T and R1: 22 Ω each from the AC-coupled earpiece drive, 100 pF to GND; S: bead + 100 pF, 2.2 kΩ
bias from MIC_VCC; TN: 1 MΩ pull-up, 1 kΩ to IO12, and the 10 kΩ tip bleed makes it read low
with no plug; R1N unused. ESD: PESD5V0S2BT ×2 (bidirectional) at the pads. Simulated in `sim/`
b04.
