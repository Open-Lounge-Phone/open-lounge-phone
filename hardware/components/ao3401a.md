## Absolute maximum vs our conditions

VGS = −VSYS ≈ −4.4 V when on (≤ 12 V) ✓; worst LED current 13 × 37 mA ≈ 0.48 A → 14 mW ✓.
With the gate pull-up of 100 kΩ the turn-off is slow (≈ 100 kΩ × Ciss, a few tens of µs,
Ciss not re-checked) — harmless for an LED rail. Inrush into 2 × 22 µF + 13 × 100 nF at turn-on
is limited only by RDS(on): check VSYS droop in H4.

## Pinout vs footprint

KiCad SOT-23 1 = G, 2 = S, 3 = D; Q2: 1 VLED_PFET_G, 2 VSYS, 3 VLED ✓ (source to the supply,
as a P-channel high-side switch needs). **Resolved.**
