# TDK PS1240P02BT — piezo ringer (BZ1)

| | |
|---|---|
| MPN / maker | PS1240P02BT (through-hole, Ø12.2 mm, 4 kHz, external drive), TDK |
| Function here | the ringer: VBUS (5 V) → piezo → Q1 collector; 1 kΩ across the piezo; IO45 → 1 kΩ → Q1 base; firmware drives a PWM tone |
| Datasheet | TDK PS series (Feb 2022): https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/b82b1254cb55646d45aaa9841e8f9db1.pdf |
| LCSC / JLC | C76871; ~$0.21; **extended** |
| Footprint | `Buzzer_Beeper:Buzzer_TDK_PS1240P02BT_D12.2mm_H6.5mm` |

The recommended drive circuit ([p2]) is exactly this: a transistor on the low side and "about
1 kΩ" across the element to charge and discharge it. Rated 70 dB at 10 cm (3 V square wave,
LCSC parameters); 5 V drive and a resonant port in the enclosure add a few dB. A piezo draws
only a few mA and needs no flyback diode.

**Open (owner):** loudness. If a louder ringer is wanted, a magnetic buzzer such as MLT-8540H
(85 dB, C95298) drops onto the same GPIO and transistor plus one flyback diode.
