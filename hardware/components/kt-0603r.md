## Pinout vs footprint (cathode resolved)

Package drawing [p2]: pin ① is marked "+" (anode) and pin ② is "−"; the green corner mark on the
package is at the ② end, and the polarity symbol shows the cathode bar at ②. So **cathode = the
end with the green mark = vendor pin ②**. KiCad `LED_0603_1608Metric`: pad 1 = K, pad 2 = A; D21
nets pad 1 = PRIV_LED_K, pad 2 = PRIV_LED_A ✓. Note the vendor numbers pins the opposite way to
KiCad (vendor 1 = anode); the assembler must place the **marked end on KiCad pad 1**. Check the
JLC rotation/polarity preview (F-28).
