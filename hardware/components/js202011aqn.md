## Pinout vs footprint

KiCad `SW_CK_JS202011AQN_DPDT_Angled`: pads 1 (0, 0), 2 (2.5, 0), 3 (5, 0), 4 (5, 3.3),
5 (2.5, 3.3), 6 (0, 3.3). Poles and commons match the datasheet (1-2-3 / 4-5-6, commons 2 and 5)
✓ — so **pole A (the privacy function) is correct regardless of the questions below**.

**Pole-B polarity (UNVERIFIED):** the C&K circuit diagram draws pin 4 above pin 1 and 6 above 3
[p1], while the KiCad footprint puts **6** opposite 1 and 4 opposite 3. If the physical part
matches the C&K diagram, the footprint's pad 6 (MUTE_SENSE) sits on the terminal that closes
together with 1-2 — i.e. MUTE_SENSE would read **low when unmuted** (inverted). The circuit
diagram is schematic, not a pin map, so this needs a continuity check on a sample (F-15). The
effect is limited to the reported state (firmware can invert); the hardware mute is unaffected.
