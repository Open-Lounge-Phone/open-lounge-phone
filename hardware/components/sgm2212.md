## Sizing (checked by `make build`, `schematic/power_budget.yaml`)

3V3 peak ≈ 450 mA (ESP32 TX 355 mA + codec/earpiece 30 + display allowance 60 + small loads)
vs 800 mA rated (≥ 20 % margin) and the module's ≥ 0.5 A supply rule. Average in a call ≈ 200 mA:
(5.25 − 3.3) V × 0.2 A = 0.39 W → Tj ≈ 86 °C at 40 °C ambient. At the lowest USB voltage
(4.40 V) the headroom above the 380 mV dropout is 0.72 V. `make sim` (b01) simulates the Wi-Fi
burst: 3V3 stays ≥ 3.13 V at the module in the pessimistic corner.

Capacitors: 10 µF at VIN (the USB attach limit is 10 µF), 2.2 µF at VOUT, 22 µF + 100 nF at the
module (Espressif [WROOM p41]). Effective output capacitance after DC bias is ≈ 14 µF, slightly
above the 10 µF of [p4] but in line with [p10] ("larger capacitance ... improve the load
transient").
