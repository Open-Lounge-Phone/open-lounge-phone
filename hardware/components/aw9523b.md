## Recommended application circuit vs ours

| Datasheet (typical application 2, keyboard) [p3] | Ours | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 4.7 kΩ I2C pull-ups, RSTN to MCU | shared 4.7 kΩ; RSTN pulled up + RC | ✓ |
| 1 µF on VCC | 100 nF | △ add 1 µF (the 3V3 pour has bulk nearby; low risk) |
| Key inputs with external pull-ups (100 k in the figure) | 10 kΩ each | ✓ (0.33 mA per pressed key) |

**Power-up behaviour (resolved):** with AD1 = AD0 = GND every port powers up as an output
driving **0** [p11]. Consequences: LED_PWR_EN starts low → LED chain unpowered ✓ (HW-FUNC-02);
until firmware configures the key ports as inputs, each 10 kΩ pull-up sinks 0.33 mA into a low
output (≈ 5 mA total for 15 inputs) — harmless, but firmware must set Config_Port0/1 first.
