## Recommended application circuit vs ours

REF2 to the protected supply rail, REF1 to ground [p1, p5-6]. D7 uses the **switched** HS_VBUS
as REF2: when the SY6280 is off, its output is discharged through 150 Ω [SY6280 p3], so the
steering diodes clamp the CC/D± lines one diode drop above ≈ 0 V. With Rp (33 kΩ to 3V3) on
CC this forward-biases a steering diode (≈ 0.1 mA into HS_VBUS) — harmless, but CC reads ≈ 0.6 V
while the port is off, and a PC-supplied VBUS in download mode will be clamped by the 6 V TVS only
above 6 V ✓. Keep in mind for F-01/F-04.
