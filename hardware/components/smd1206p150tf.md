## Absolute maximum vs our conditions

| Parameter | Rating | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Hold current at 40 °C | 1.34 A | charger input limit up to **1.56 A** (K_ILIM max [BQ24074 p12]) + handset port up to 0.45–0.57 A direct from VBUS (the budget's worst uncapped case is 1.79 A) → can trip under a sustained worst-case load in a warm enclosure ✗ (F-07) |
| V_max | 8 V | USB ≤ 5.25 V ✓; a faulty > 8 V source can damage the PTC before the TVS/OVP path settles |
| Drop | 0.12 Ω max × 1.3 A = 0.16 V | reduces VBUS at the charger and the handset port (F-05) |

## Pinout vs footprint

`Fuse:Fuse_1206_3216Metric`, non-polar: 1 VBUS_C, 2 VBUS ✓.

## Recommended application circuit vs ours

PTC before the TVS so the TVS cannot be destroyed by a sustained overvoltage — ✓ as placed.
