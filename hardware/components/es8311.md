## Our circuit (the [p4] parts plus the handset interface)

Earpiece: OUTP (biased at VMID) → 22 µF → 22 Ω → tip + ring 1 of the jack (both earpiece
contacts carry the signal, a mono handset uses one); 4.7 kΩ tip-to-GND keeps the coupling cap
at 0 V and doubles as the insertion-detect reference. Corner 134 Hz into 32 + 22 Ω. OUTN unused.
Mic: 3V3 → 1 kΩ / 10 µF filter (16 Hz) → 2.2 kΩ electret bias → sleeve; sleeve → 1 µF →
MIC1P; MIC1N → 1 µF → GND (pseudo-differential per [p4]). There is no MICBIAS pin on the
ES8311, hence the RC-filtered bias from 3V3.
