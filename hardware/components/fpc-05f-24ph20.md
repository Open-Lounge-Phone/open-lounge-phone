## FPC contact side (resolved, with one condition)

The panel drawing's side view labels the tail's **"CONTACT SIDE"** on the same face as the
display surface, with the stiffener on the opposite face [GDEY029T94 p7]. Our placement folds the
tail 180° under the panel into J6 (placement.yaml "tail folds under"), which turns the contacts
to face **down**, toward the PCB → a **bottom-contact** connector is correct. If the tail were
routed without the fold (connector beside the panel), a top-contact connector would be needed.
Confirm with a sample (fold radius and tail length 9.56–14.4 mm from the glass [p7]).
