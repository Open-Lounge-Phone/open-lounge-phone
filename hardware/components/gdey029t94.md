pull-down, 2.2 Ω sense, 3 × MBR0530, C3/C4 4.7 µF/25 V, 1 µF/25 V on VSH2, VCI, VDD, VSH1, VGH,
VSL, VGL, VCOM (ours 1 µF/50 V) — **identical topology and values** except the inductor current
rating (see inductors-ferrites.md) ✓.
