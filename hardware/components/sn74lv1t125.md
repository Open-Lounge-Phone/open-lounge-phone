At VCC = VSYS 4.4 V (below the 4.5 V row) VIH is between the 3.3 V and 4.5 V rows (≈ 1.4–2.0 V
[p1, p6]); a 3.3 V GPIO high (≥ 2.64 V) clears it with margin ✓. On battery (VSYS 3.0–4.2 V)
the output follows VCC, and the SK6812s need VDD ≥ 3.7 V anyway.

## Recommended application circuit vs ours

0.1 µF at VCC (standard) — ours 100 nF ✓. Series 330 Ω limits back-feed into an unpowered LED
chain (VLED switched off while U5 still drives) — ✓ design choice (SCHEMATIC deviation 9).
