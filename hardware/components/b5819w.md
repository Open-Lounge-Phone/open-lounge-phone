## Pinout vs footprint

`Diode_SMD:D_SOD-123`: pad 1 = K, pad 2 = A. D8: 1 HS_VIN, 2 VBUS ✓; D9: 1 HS_VIN, 2 VSYS ✓.

## Recommended application circuit vs ours

Diode-OR of two rails is a standard use. The cost is the forward drop: HS_VIN ≈ VBUS − 0.3…0.45 V
on USB and ≈ VBAT − 0.3 V on battery, before the PTC and SY6280 drops.
