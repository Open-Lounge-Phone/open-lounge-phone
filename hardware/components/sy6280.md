# SY6280AAC — current-limited power switch, handset-port VBUS (U15)

| | |
|---|---|
| MPN / maker | SY6280AAC (SOT-23-5), Silergy |
| Function here | Switches HS_VIN (diode-OR of VBUS and VSYS via two B5819W) to HS_VBUS on the handset USB-C port. EN = HS_VBUS_EN (GPIO3, 100 k pull-down). R_SET 15 kΩ → 0.45 A limit. |
| Requirements | HW-FUNC-06; HW-ELEC-19; HW-PRIV-03; HW-SAFE-03 |
| Datasheet | Silergy **AN_SY6280 Rev 0.1 (2011-01-13), "Preliminary Spec"**: https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0271e5b2ca2a46b8bb16f65855f12fe2.pdf |
| LCSC / JLC | C55136 — LCSC 131 410, JLC 135 169 (2026-09-29); $0.092 @1, $0.055 @1k; **extended** |
| Lifecycle | UNVERIFIED — only a preliminary 2011 application note is published; widely stocked. |
| Alternates | Candidates with published full datasheets (pinout UNVERIFIED): TI TPS2553DBV (adjustable limit, SOT-23-6 — different footprint), AP2553 family. |
| SPICE (H4) | None. **Behavioural**: switch with R_on 80 mΩ, constant-current limit 6800/R_SET (±25 %), fold-back 50 % into a short, EN threshold 0.8/2.0 V, 150 Ω output discharge when off, no body diode. |

## Absolute maximum vs our conditions

| Parameter | Abs max [p2] | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| All pins | 6 V | HS_VIN ≤ 5.25 − 0.3 V; a PC feeding 5.25 V into the port while off ✓ |
| Power dissipation | 0.6 W (θJA 200 °C/W) | 0.45 A² × 0.08 Ω = 16 mW normal; in current limit into a short ≈ 4.7 V × 0.23 A ≈ 1.1 W → thermal cycling (acceptable, self-protecting) |
