## Pinout vs footprint

`SOIC-16_3.9x9.9mm_P1.27mm` (U16) nets: 1 GND, 2 TXD → U0RXD, 3 RXD ← U0TXD, 4 V3 = 3V3,
5 UD+ = USB_DP, 6 UD− = USB_DN, 7 NC open, 8 OUT# open, 9–12 CTS/DSR/RI/DCD open, 13 DTR# →
Q6 base network, 14 RTS# → Q5 base network, 15 R232 open, 16 VCC = 3V3. ✓ Matches the SOP-16
table [p3]. (R232 floats: the datasheet does not state its internal pull — UNVERIFIED; the
existing parts.py note says "built-in pull-down".)

## Recommended application circuit vs ours

Datasheet 3.3 V circuit: V3 = VCC = 3.3 V with 0.1 µF [p5] — ours matches (100 nF). Standard
DTR/RTS two-transistor auto-reset — matches the Espressif DevKit pattern.
