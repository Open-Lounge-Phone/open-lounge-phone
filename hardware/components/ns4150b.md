| Korvo: 0.1 µF + 150 kΩ input, 22 pF on the DAC side, 10 k CTRL pull-down, VCC = 5 V | 100 nF + 150 kΩ, 100 k CTRL pull-down, VCC = VSYS 4.4 V | gain and supply lower than the 3 W rating assumes |

**Output power check (F-06):** ES8311 full scale at AVDD 3.0 V is 0.91 Vrms differential
[ES8311 p9]. With A_V = 1.6 the amplifier output is ≈ 1.45 Vrms → **≈ 0.53 W into 4 Ω**,
far below the 3 W part rating and the 470–550 mA "ringing max" rows of the power budget. A lower
R_in (e.g. 47 kΩ → A_V 5.1) would reach clipping at VSYS 4.4 V (≈ 1.6 W at 1 % THD scaled from
[p3]); whether the gain definition is single-ended or differential is UNVERIFIED (Chinese text
only) — measure in EVT.
