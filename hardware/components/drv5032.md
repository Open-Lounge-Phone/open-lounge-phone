Field check (PROTO_BOX): on-hook ≈ 70 mT at 2.0 mm — far above BOP max 4.8 mT ✓. Off-hook
≈ **1.6 mT** at 10 mm. The switch releases only when |B| falls **below BRP**, and BRP can be as
low as **0.5 mT** [p6]: a part at the low end of BRP would **never report off-hook** with this
plunger travel. Off-hook |B| must be < 0.5 mT for every part (≈ 3× margin on the typical value):
more travel, a weaker or smaller magnet, or a larger sensor-to-magnet gap. **F-26** (HW-FUNC-08).
