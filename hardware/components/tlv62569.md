Output voltage: V_OUT = 0.6 V × (1 + 100 k/22 k) = **3.327 V** nominal; with VFB limits and 1 %
resistors 3.26–3.41 V. Worst-case dropout on a 3.4 V battery at 0.6 A: 3.4 − 0.6 × (0.1 +
0.052 DCR [SWPA p7]) = 3.31 V — the rail follows the battery near empty; the module minimum
3.0 V [WROOM p27] is reached at VBAT ≈ 3.1 V → firmware shutdown threshold ≥ 3.3 V.
