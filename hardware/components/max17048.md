## Absolute maximum vs our conditions

| Parameter | Abs max [p2] | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| CELL to GND | −0.3 … 12 V | VBAT ≤ 4.2 V ✓ |
| All other pins | −0.3 … 6 V | VDD ≤ 4.2 V; SDA/SCL 3.3 V ✓ |
| SDA/ALRT sink | 20 mA | < 1 mA ✓ |

With no pack fitted VBAT is not driven (the charger's TS fault keeps it from charging), so the
gauge is unpowered. Its SCL/SDA have no clamp to VDD in the ratings (−0.3 … 6 V to GND) and only
internal pull-downs [p6], so an unpowered gauge does not load the bus beyond those pull-downs
(value not stated — UNVERIFIED; check I2C levels with no pack in EVT).
