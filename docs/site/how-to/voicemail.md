Every call that isn't answered can go to voicemail — to a grown-up, to a kid's phone, and to
people on other servers.

## When a call goes to voicemail

- **No answer:** the call rings for 25 seconds (you can change it, see below), then the caller
  hears the greeting and a tone.
- **Declined or busy:** straight to the greeting.
- **Quiet hours, not taking calls, or offline:** straight to the greeting — nothing rings.

The caller talks after the tone and **hangs up to send**; a message can be up to two minutes. In
the companion app that's the **Hang up & send** button; on a phone it's putting the handset
down. A call that was never allowed (someone who isn't on a kid's phone's list, or a kid's phone
during quiet hours calling out) doesn't get voicemail either: the rules about who may call whom
don't change.

## Where messages go

- **To you:** your own **Voicemail** tab in the companion (with a transcript when the server has
  speech-to-text), and "missed" on your own phone if you have one.
- **To a kid's phone:** the guardians' **Voicemail** tab, and the phone shows **MISSED <name>**
  until a grown-up has listened.
- **To someone at a Lounge phone:** their own inbox — never the shared phone.
- **Across servers:** the message is delivered to the other person's own server, through your
  connection.

## Your greeting

Open **Voicemail** and tap **Greeting** at the top. There are three choices:

- **Standard greeting** — callers hear "*Your name* can't take your call. Leave a message after
  the tone."
- **Record just your name** (up to 3 seconds) — your own voice says your name inside the same
  sentence: "*(your recording)* can't take your call. Leave a message after the tone."
- **Record a whole greeting** (up to 30 seconds) — exactly what you say.

You can play it back, re-record it, or go back to the standard greeting at any time. The same
card sets **how long calls ring** before voicemail (10 to 60 seconds).

People on other servers hear your greeting too, but only people you're connected with: their
server asks yours for it, and yours checks the connection first.

## A kid's phone's greeting

Guardians set it on the phone's **Manage** page, recording it in the app. A kids' phone never
records the child: the child can't record its greeting on the phone, and a call the child makes
that isn't answered ends without a message (there's nothing to leave). Messages left **for** the
child's phone by the people allowed to call it work as usual; guardians hear them in the app.

## How long it's kept, and transcripts

A space's guardians choose how long its voicemail and call history are kept (30 days, 1 year or
forever) and whether voicemail is transcribed: **Settings → Privacy in this space**. With
transcription off, messages are never sent to speech-to-text. For people you're connected with,
your own choice comes first: open someone under **Connections → History** to see your calls and
their messages to you, and to set how long you keep them.
