A **team** or **organization** space turns Open Lounge Phone into a small office phone system:
everyone gets a short **extension**, callers reach a **ring group** like "Front desk" or
"Support", and admins see the **call log**. Homes (families and kids' phones) don't change.
There's still no connection to the regular phone network — people reach you by extension, by
your address `you@your.server`, or as a connection.

## Start a team

In the companion app, **Add a household** → choose **Team** or **Organization**. You're its
**owner**. Invite people from **People**: a **member** (in the directory, can call and be
called) or, as the owner, an **admin** (can also manage people, extensions, ring groups and
hours). Only the owner makes or removes admins.

## The directory and extensions

Open **Directory**. Everyone in the space sees the people, desk phones, rooms and ring groups,
with their extensions, and can search by name, address or number. Type a number under **Dial an
extension** and press **Call**.

Admins hand out numbers under **Admin → Extensions**: 2 to 6 digits, e.g. `201` for a person,
`210` for the kitchen's Lounge phone, `250` for the standup room.

**On a phone:** press **MENU**, then **6 Dial ext**, type the digits (BACK deletes one) and press
**MENU** to dial. The phone says the numbers as you type and tells you if there's no such
extension.

## Ring groups

**Admin → Ring groups → New ring group**: a name, its extension, its members, and how it rings:

- **Everyone at once** — the first to pick up takes it;
- **One after another** — in the order you ticked them, each for the ring time you set;
- **Take turns (round robin)** — like one after another, but each call starts with the next
  person.

Someone who isn't taking calls or is already on a call is skipped, and declining only stops your
own phone ringing. If nobody answers, the caller can leave a message in the group's **shared
voicemail box**.

## Business hours

**Admin → Business hours**: the days and times the space is open. A ring group can have its
own. Outside those hours a call to a group goes to its voicemail box — or, if you choose, to
another group (say, "Night line") or to one person. A call is forwarded only once, so it never
bounces around. Calls to a person always follow that person's own "taking calls" switch.

## Shared voicemail

Messages for a ring group show up in the **Voicemail** tab of each of its members (and the
admins), marked "in Support's shared box". The first person who plays one marks it **heard by**
them, so nobody calls the same customer back twice.

## Transferring calls

Press **Transfer** during a call and pick a person, or type an extension. In a team or
organization you can transfer anyone — even a caller from another household or another server —
to your own space's people, phones, ring groups and extensions. Their server hands the call
over. (A kids' phone is never transferred: its own server ends the call instead.)

## Call log and audit trail

Admins find **Admin → Call log** (every call in the space, with **Export CSV**) and **Admin →
Audit trail**, also under **Settings → Changes** (who changed what: extensions, groups, hours,
roles, invites, phones, privacy, the space's name and time zone; never what anyone said or
wrote). Both are kept as long as the space's history setting (**Privacy**). The owner and admins
can rename the space and change its time zone under **Settings**; business hours follow.
