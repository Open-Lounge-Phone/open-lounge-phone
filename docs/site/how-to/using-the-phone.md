An Open Lounge Phone is meant to be obvious to anyone who has used a desk phone: lift the
handset, press a key, talk, hang up. This page explains everything else, from what the light is
telling you to how to start over. It describes the hardware phone; the [browser phone](/getting-started/)
on an old tablet or laptop works the same way, with on-screen keys and a big handset button.

## The keys

Twelve keys in two rows:

```
 1   2   3   4   5   MENU
 6   7   8   9   0   BACK
```

- **1 to 9 and 0 are your people.** Each digit key calls whoever is on it. The names live on the
  keycaps (print them from the app: **Manage phone → Print key labels**) and on the screen when
  you lift the handset.
- **MENU** opens the menu from anywhere, even in a call (then it opens the call screen).
- **BACK** steps out of a menu, one level at a time.
- **The hook** is the switch the handset rests on. Lifting the handset is how you start a call
  and how you answer one; putting it down hangs up.

There's no dial pad for numbers, no Enter and no End key: nothing to get wrong.

## Making and taking calls

**To call someone:** lift the handset. You'll hear a dial tone and the screen shows who is on
each key (two columns, like the key rows). Press their key. You'll hear it ringing; when they
answer, talk. Hang up to end the call.

**When it rings:** the screen says who's calling (`GRANDMA CALLING`) and the light flashes.
Lift the handset to answer. If you don't, the caller may be offered your voicemail. To stop the
ringing without answering, press **BACK** (the screen reminds you: `BACK: SILENCE`): the light
keeps flashing, and the call goes to voicemail at the usual time.

**If a call doesn't go through,** the screen says why, so nobody has to guess: `BUSY`,
`NO ANSWER`, `DECLINED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `NOT ALLOWED` (that person isn't on this phone's list) or
`CALL FAILED`, or `CONNECTION LOST` when the call's connection dropped and couldn't be
restored. Press another key to try someone else. On a Lounge phone nobody is signed in to,
a public line says `SIGN IN TO CALL`: only someone signed in can call one.

**Leaving a message:** when someone doesn't answer and voicemail is on, you'll see
`LEAVE A MESSAGE`, then hear a beep. Speak after it and hang up to send it (up to 2 minutes).
`MESSAGE SENT` confirms it.

**During a call,** press **MENU** for the call screen:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hold the call (1 again resumes) |
| 2 | Transfer: then press the key of the person who should get the call |
| 3 | Mute (3 again unmutes): the other side hears silence. Also in a room |
| 9 | Volume |

While you talk, the screen reminds you how: `MENU 3: MUTE` takes turns with the caller's name.
Muted, it says `MUTED` and the light blinks slowly in pairs.

Adding a third person and merging calls happen in the app or the browser phone; the hardware
phone carries one call's audio at a time.

**If the connection drops** (a Wi-Fi blip), the screen says `RECONNECTING` instead of leaving
you in silence, and the call carries on once it's back. If it can't come back within about
20 seconds, the call ends on both sides with `CONNECTION LOST`.

## Call waiting

When someone calls while you're already in a call, you won't miss it: you hear a soft beep in
the earpiece every few seconds and the screen says `WAITING: MOM` / `1 TAKE 2 IGNORE`.

- **1** takes the new call. On the hardware phone this ends the call you're in (it carries one
  call's audio at a time). In the app and on the browser phone, the first call goes on hold
  instead, and you can go back to it, merge the two or transfer one.
- **2** ignores it: it stops beeping, and the caller is offered your voicemail at the usual
  time.
- Hanging up the call you're in makes the waiting call ring like any call.

Kids' phones get call waiting too, with the same rules as any call (only people on their list,
and not in quiet hours). It's on unless you turn it off: for yourself in the app's settings, for
a kid's phone on its page in the app.

## Missed calls

A call that rang and nobody answered (and the caller didn't leave a message) is a missed call.
The ready screen shows it (`MISSED MOM` / `MENU 5: MISSED`; with several, `3 MISSED CALLS` and
each name in turn) and the light breathes slowly until you've looked.

Press **MENU 5** to go through them, newest first: who, how many times and when
(`2 CALLS 14:05`). With the handset up, **1** calls the shown person back, **2** shows the next
one and **3** clears them all. On your own phone the list covers everything that rang for you,
on any of your phones and apps; clearing it on one clears it everywhere. A kid's phone can only
call back people it's allowed to call: anyone else gets `NOT ALLOWED`.

The app has the same list on its Home screen, with **Call back**.

## Redial

Lift the handset and press **MENU 8** to call again whoever this phone called last (a key, an
extension or a call back). The phone remembers it even after a restart.

## The screen

The screen always shows what's going on and, on its second line, what you can do next. A few
you'll see often:

| Screen | Means |
|---|---|
| `READY 14:05` / `ANA'S PHONE` | Ready, with the time and whose phone it is (`KIDS: SMITHS`, `LOUNGE: STUDIO`) |
| `MISSED MOM` / `PLAY IN APP` | A missed call with a message waiting. On a kids' phone: `ASK A GROWN-UP` |
| `MISSED MOM` / `MENU 5: MISSED` | A missed call, no message (see [Missed calls](#missed-calls)) |
| `3 MISSED CALLS` | Several missed calls; the names take turns |
| `MISSED CALL BACK` / `ACME SUPPORT` | A [public line](/how-to/public-lines/#call-me-back) called you back and nobody answered |
| `QUIET TIL 07:00` | Quiet hours: it won't ring until then |
| `IN CALL 01:23` / name | In a call (`REC 01:23` if the call is being recorded, `MUTED 01:23` when you've muted it) |
| `RECONNECTING` / name | In a call whose connection dropped: it's being restored |
| `WAITING: MOM` / `1 TAKE 2 IGNORE` | Another call is waiting (see [Call waiting](#call-waiting)) |
| `CHECK IN BY 10:00` / `PRESS 1: I'M OK` | The daily check-in (see [Daily check-in](#daily-check-in)) |
| `OFFLINE` / `RECONNECTING` | Wi-Fi or the server dropped out; it keeps trying by itself |
| `JOINING WI-FI`, `WI-FI DROPPED` | Joining the network (once at power-on is usually just timing), or a working connection was lost |
| `WRONG PASSWORD?`, `WEAK WI-FI`, `NETWORK NOT SEEN` | Why Wi-Fi won't join. `NETWORK NOT SEEN` usually means a 5 GHz-only network: the phone needs 2.4 GHz |
| `SET UP:` / `CHOOSE A SERVER` | A new phone: see [Set up a phone](/how-to/pair-a-phone/) |
| `PAIR 123 456` | Enter this code in the app to add the phone |

Long names scroll. The screen is e-paper: it keeps the last picture even without power, so a
phone that's unplugged still looks "on". The light tells you whether it really is.

## The light

One red light on the face of the phone.

| Light | Means |
|---|---|
| Dim | Ready |
| Slow breathing | Ready, and you have missed calls or new messages |
| Pulsing | Pairing, or connecting to the server |
| Fast flashing | Ringing, or a Lounge phone waiting for you to press the key it shows |
| A short blink every 2 seconds | Offline |
| On | Calling, in a call or in a room |
| Slow blinks in pairs | In a call, muted |
| Double flash every second | **This call is being recorded** |
| Off | No power |

## The sounds

The ringer is a small piezo in the base: it rings, clicks when you press a key, beeps three
times when a pairing code appears, twice when something is done, and once (long) for an error.
In the earpiece you'll hear the familiar tones: dial tone, ringing, busy, a beep when a
message starts recording, and a soft beep every few seconds when another call is waiting. Spoken prompts on the hardware phone are planned; the browser phone
speaks them already.

## The menu

Press **MENU**, then the number. The menu shows only what applies to your phone and closes by
itself after 20 seconds.

| Key | Item | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Volume** | Earpiece: 1 quieter, 2 louder. Ringer: 4 soft / medium / loud, 5 the ring style (classic, double, beeps); each press plays it |
| 2 | **Voicemail** | How many new messages. On your own phone: lift the handset and press **1** to play them (1 again, 2 next, BACK stop), **3** to mark them all heard. A message played to the end counts as heard everywhere. On a kids' phone a grown-up listens in the app |
| 3 | **Network** | The Wi-Fi network, its signal and the server. **1 Change** opens the setup network to pick another Wi-Fi network or server |
| 4 | **Intercom** or **Chime** | When they're on for this phone: call another room (see [House intercom](#house-intercom)), or send the phone's chime to every phone (see [Chime](#chime)) |
| 5 | **Missed calls** | Who called while you didn't answer: **1** call back, **2** next, **3** clear (see [Missed calls](#missed-calls)). On a [Lounge phone](/how-to/lounge-phone/) you're signed in to: **Open to chat**, to let people see you're around |
| 6 | **Extension** | In a [team or organization](/how-to/workplace-phone-system/): dial an extension, then MENU to call |
| 7 | **Phone check** | Everything about the connection on one page; **1** starts the self-test. With the handset up it also shows the handset mic's live level: speak into it |
| 8 | **Redial** | With the handset up: call again whoever this phone called last. On a Lounge phone: **Log out**, and the phone forgets you |
| 9 | **Update** | Install a new version now (official firmware with updates on) |
| 0 | **About** | The firmware, board and server, and the phone's four fingerprint words |

## Home features

Three extras for a home, each off until a guardian turns it on for a phone in the app
(**Manage phone → Home features**).

### Daily check-in

For someone living alone, such as a grandparent. Pick a time (say 10:00). Each day until then
the phone asks `CHECK IN BY 10:00` / `PRESS 1: I'M OK`; pressing **1** says they're fine
(`THANK YOU` / `CHECKED IN`). If nobody has pressed it by the time, every guardian of the
household gets an alert in their voicemail inbox ("Gran didn't check in by 10:00."), once that
day, and the phone shows `CHECK-IN MISSED` until someone presses 1. The server keeps the time, so
a phone that's unplugged or offline is noticed too.

### Chime

"Dinner's ready": give a phone a chime (up to two short lines) and **MENU 4**, then **1**, shows
the words on every phone of the household with a chime, like a notice posted from the app. It lasts 15 minutes; a phone sends at most one a minute.

### House intercom

Phones of the household on the same Wi-Fi call each other room to room, with no server and no
internet in between. Turn it on for each phone that should join; the room's name is the phone's
name. Lift the handset, press **MENU 4**, then the room's digit (**0** rings every room, and the
first to lift answers). The other phone rings with `KITCHEN CALLING`; lifting it talks. Hanging
up either side ends it.

Only your household's phones can ring each other: every message is signed with a key the server
gives your phones, and the audio between two phones is encrypted. Neighbours on the same Wi-Fi
can't ring your rooms or listen in, though they could see that two phones are talking. It works
on the hardware phone (a browser can't do it).

## When something's wrong

- **It says `OFFLINE`.** Give it a minute: it reconnects by itself. If it stays offline, press
  **MENU 7** (phone check): it shows the Wi-Fi signal and the last problem in plain words. A weak
  signal usually means the antenna or the distance to the router.
- **It won't join a new Wi-Fi network.** Press **MENU 3**, then **1 Change**. The screen shows
  a QR code: scan it with your phone's camera, and a setup page opens where you pick the network
  and enter the password.
- **The other person can't hear you.** Check you haven't muted the call (`MUTED` on the screen:
  **MENU 3** unmutes). Then lift the handset and press **MENU 7**: the page shows the mic's level
  as you speak. If it doesn't move, check the handset's plug.
- **A key or the handset seems dead.** Press **MENU 7**, then **1**, for the self-test: press
  every key, lift and replace the handset and unplug and replug it. Each one ticks off on the
  screen, and you'll hear a tone in the earpiece. `ALL GOOD` at the end means the hardware is
  fine.
- **You can't reach the menu** (a Lounge phone hides the network settings). Hold **MENU and
  BACK** while you plug the phone in: after 3 seconds it opens the setup network.
- **Start over completely.** Hold **MENU and BACK** while plugging it in and keep holding for
  10 seconds. The phone erases everything (Wi-Fi, server, its key) and starts like new. To
  move it to another family or use it another way, remove it in the app instead: it wipes itself
  the same way.
