Keys: 1 2 3 4 5 MENU over 6 7 8 9 0 BACK. Lift the handset and press a digit to speed-dial; MENU opens the menu (digits pick what the display shows, BACK steps out). Keyboard: Space lifts or hangs up the handset, 0–9 press digits, M = MENU, Esc/Backspace = BACK.
