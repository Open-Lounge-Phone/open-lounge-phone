## What works today

- **Calls** between the phone and the grown-ups' companion app, peer to peer (audio never passes
  through the server).
- **Default-deny contacts:** a guardian decides exactly who can call the phone and who it can call.
- **Quiet hours** per household, with a clear "quiet until 7:00" on the phone.
- **Voicemail** recorded by the caller, with automatic transcripts for the grown-ups.
- **Invites and passkeys:** add family by link; sign in with a passkey, no passwords or email.
- **Two ways to run it:** your own Cloudflare account (Workers, Durable Objects, D1, R2) or a
  self-hosted Node server with SQLite.

## The hardware (in design)

One open circuit board built around an ESP32-S3:
- 12 swappable mechanical keys with lights, and a small e-ink status strip
- a speaker and a microphone, NFC for tap-to-pair, and an optional battery
- a USB-C port for an off-the-shelf classic G-style handset

It sits in a simple 3D-printable base, and you pick the colour, keycaps and cord. Schematic,
layout and enclosure are all code in the repository, so anyone can build or modify one. Follow
along in [Build the hardware](/how-to/build-the-hardware/).
