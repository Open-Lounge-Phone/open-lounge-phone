The public hub, `hub.openloungephone.app`, is **free, funded by donations** — no paid plan, no
ads, no tracking. It will always be free to try the service, and it's live at
[hub.openloungephone.app](https://hub.openloungephone.app).

<Funding variant="full" />

## Why it's cheap

Most calls are **peer to peer**: the audio goes directly between the two people and never touches
the hub. Only about **20 % of calls** need Cloudflare's relay (both people behind strict
networks), at roughly **$0.04 per 1,000 relayed minutes**, and phones use Opus DTX so silence
costs almost nothing. The rest is small: requests, Durable Objects that sleep when nobody is
calling, database rows and voicemail storage. The full cost table is on the
[hub page](/project/hub/).

A generous monthly **fair-use allowance** keeps the hub fair for everyone. It stops abuse; calls
in progress are never cut off, and your own server has no allowance at all.

## For full assurance, run your own

The hub is the easy way in. For **full security assurance, run your own server** on your own
domain — [on your Cloudflare account](/how-to/deploy-cloudflare/) or
[with Docker](/how-to/self-host/): your data, your keys, still federated with everyone.
