Open Lounge Phone

This is an Open Lounge Phone server. Open Lounge Phone is a screen-light, open-source phone for kids, homes and lounges: real keys and a handset, calls only between people who accepted each other, quiet hours and voicemail.

People with an account here sign in with a passkey to call, manage their phones and see voicemail. Any old phone, tablet or laptop can be the phone at /device/. Servers connect to each other, so people on different servers can reach each other by address (name@server).
