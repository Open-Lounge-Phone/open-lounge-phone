# Open Lounge Phone

**A screen-light phone for kids and lounges: open source, self-hosted, no spam.**

Mechanical keys, a real handset, a small e-ink strip, and nothing else.

- **Kids phone:** it can only call, and be called by, the people a guardian approves. Quiet hours
  send callers to voicemail with a transcript instead of waking anyone up.
- **Grown-ups:** call each other from the companion app, or from any old phone or tablet used as a
  phone.
- **Lounge phone:** a shared phone for venues and offices. Sign in with your app and a key press, and
  it forgets you when you leave.
- **Your own server, or the free hub:** run it on your own Cloudflare account or with Docker, or try
  it on the donation-funded public hub. Servers federate, so people on different servers can connect
  (knock, then talk).

Most calls are peer to peer and encrypted end to end. The server never hears them.
